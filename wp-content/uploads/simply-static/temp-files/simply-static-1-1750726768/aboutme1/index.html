About Me

Simas Alaune 🇱🇹

simas.alaune@gmail.com

From a young age, I was always interested in video games. Whether it was watching my older brother and dad play Jak & Daxter on the PlayStation 2 or getting PK’ed in Mu Online and Lineage 2. 

After realizing I could study game-development by combining my two favourite high-school subjects – IT & Art – I enrolled in the Multimedia Technologies program at Kaunas University of Technology (KTU) in Lithuania. I graduated with top marks, received scholarships and decided to continue my studies at the IT University of Copenhagen in Denmark, driven by my dream of living abroad and pushing myself further.

In game development, I’m most drawn to the visual side. Combining my tech and artistic skills, I always aim to create original and visually distinct experiences in the indie game-dev world.
