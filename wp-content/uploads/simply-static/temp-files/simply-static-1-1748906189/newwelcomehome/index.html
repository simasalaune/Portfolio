About

Welcome Home is a psychological horror game that immerses you in the unsettling life of a secluded village preparing for its annual festival. Experience a chilling tale of tradition, trust, and terror, narrated through your journey of uncovering dark secrets and making choices that determine your fate.

| Key Features

  • PSX Style
  • 12 NPCs
  • Stealth & Survival
  • Multiple Endings

Game Design

As the Game Design Lead, I was responsible for shaping the player’s journey and building tension through structured pacing, interactive mechanics, and narrative design.

| Day Cycle

  • Developed a 3-day narrative arc with scripted tasks, restricted exploration, and progressive escalation.
  • Balanced player freedom with narrative control using gated environments and task-based progression.
  • Introduced branching outcomes based on player actions, encouraging replayability.

| Jumpscares

  • Designed over 6 types of jumpscares (visual, auditory, ambient) timed to both scripted and emergent player behavior.

  • Built a psychological horror rhythm: rising tension → fake relief → payoff.

  • Integrated jumpscares into gameplay logic (e.g., curiosity punished with a scare after exploring forbidden areas).

| Inventory & Environmental Storytelling

  • Created a minimal inventory system for keys, notes, and interactive items with in-world logic.
  • Designed 10+ story clues and pickups, each contributing to worldbuilding and mystery.
  • Strategically placed props like crucified animals or half-burnt photos to hint at backstory without exposition.

| Level Design

  • Designed stealth zones with noise mechanics, vision cones, and contextual feedback (e.g., stepping on sticks increases alert meter).

  • Created hiding scenarios with high-stakes decision-making (stay hidden or escape early).

  • Directed the final escape route as a culmination of earlier gameplay: evasion, stealth, and fast-paced decision-making.
